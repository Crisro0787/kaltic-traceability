# Product thesis and evidence boundaries

## What changed

The prior argument jumped from regulatory complexity to product usefulness. Those are not equivalent. The revised presentation proposes one job: a tomato grower answers a packer’s request for the origin, dates, quantity and recipient of a harvest. It explicitly tests whether K’altic does that job better than the existing spreadsheet or supplier portal. The entire financial expansion, market extrapolation and return model have been removed.

## Supported facts

The FDA cucumber outbreak source establishes real traceability stakes, not a software market or a preventable incident attributable to this product. The retained legal sources identify document and reviewer responsibilities; none requires buying K’altic or using a blockchain. Additional FDA traceability enforcement timing does not suspend existing import or supplier-verification obligations.

`docs/architecture.md` describes farm references, field registration, and harvest records with identifiers, commodity, variety, quantity/unit, dates, farm, field and immediate subsequent recipient. The documented MVP also produces hashes, passport views and Preprod anchors. It does not establish packing-lot integration or a packer-ready export. Public transactions demonstrate technical anchoring, not business adoption, identity verification or truthful inputs.

## Hypotheses, not findings

Mariana, delivery D-018, harvest H-018, Field 02, the date, Roma variety and 1,200 kg are illustrative. No interview supplied the request. The reconstruction workflow is a baseline to investigate, not a claim that Mexican farmers universally use paper or cannot manage spreadsheets.

A stable harvest reference and reusable farm information might reduce repeated entry. A handoff accepted by the packer might reduce reconciliation. Neither benefit has been measured. A download/CSV, delivery reference and intake link are proposed additions. The grower’s willingness to pay MXN 100/month and the affordability of supporting that account remain unverified.

## What would reject the thesis

If the current spreadsheet or packer portal already handles the task well, another system may add work. If the packer will not accept the output, a better-looking record has no operational value. If staff continue duplicate entry, the workflow has not earned its place. If no party values the improvement enough to pay its delivery cost, the subscription hypothesis fails.

## Validation

With informed permission, observe an actual grower–packer handoff, collect their request format and identify who performs each step. Compare current and proposed workflows on comparable tasks: preparation/retrieval time, missing fields, corrections, duplicate entry, output acceptance and repeat use. Test payment only after use. Do not fabricate thresholds or results. Avoid uploading private farm or buyer data to public sources or chains.

The retained sources were reviewed on 18 September 2026. Crop, route and entity scope matter; this is not a complete export checklist or legal advice. The source ledger keeps the 2028 FDA timing qualification and separates official authorization from supporting origin records.
